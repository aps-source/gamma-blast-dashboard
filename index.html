Dhan does not expose historical intraday per-strike OI/volume, so this strategy is forward-tested live rather than backtested: entry signals need ~21 ticks (roughly 5+ hours at the 15-min cadence) of history on the same squeeze strike before they can fire at all, and each stored bar uses a single LTP snapshot as its O/H/L/C (no true intrabar range is available for the option premium).
